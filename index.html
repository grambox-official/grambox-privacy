GRAMBOX Listing Manager Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 21, 2026

Welcome to GRAMBOX Listing Manager.

GRAMBOX is committed to protecting personal information and respecting the privacy of users who authorize our application to access eBay services.

This Privacy Policy describes how information may be collected, used, protected, and handled in connection with GRAMBOX Listing Manager.

1. Information We Collect

When you authorize GRAMBOX Listing Manager through eBay OAuth, our application may access information according to the permissions you grant.

  • eBay seller account identifiers
  • Product listing details and item information
  • Inventory and listing-management information
  • OAuth authorization credentials
  • Information necessary to operate authorized eBay API features

We limit access to information needed for authorized application functionality.

2. How We Use Information

Information obtained through eBay APIs may be used for the following purposes:

  • Managing authorized eBay product listings
  • Supporting inventory and item information management
  • Providing functionality requested by the account owner
  • Maintaining application security and functionality
  • Meeting applicable eBay API requirements

We do not sell eBay user information or use it for unauthorized advertising.

3. eBay API and OAuth Authorization

GRAMBOX Listing Manager uses official eBay APIs and OAuth authorization mechanisms.

eBay account owners grant access through eBay's authorization process.

We do not require users to disclose their eBay account passwords directly to our application.

Authorized access is limited by the permissions granted through eBay.

4. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to help protect information processed by our application.

These measures include encrypted HTTPS communications and restricted access to application credentials.

Our current eBay account-deletion notification receiver processes verified notifications in memory and does not intentionally store notification payloads in a database.

Any additional data storage introduced for future application features will require appropriate security and retention practices.

5. Sharing of Information

GRAMBOX does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate our application, including:

  • eBay, for API access and account authorization
  • Cloudflare, for application infrastructure and security
  • GitHub Pages, for hosting this public privacy policy

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Personal information will be retained only as necessary for authorized application operations or applicable legal requirements.

Users may request information about personal data held by GRAMBOX and request deletion where applicable.

We respect eBay account deletion notifications and applicable data protection requirements.

7. User Rights

Depending on applicable law, users may request:

  • Access to personal information
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion of personal information
  • Information about how their data is processed

Users may also revoke application permissions through their eBay account settings.

8. Cookies and Tracking

This privacy policy page does not intentionally use advertising cookies or tracking scripts.

Hosting and infrastructure providers may process standard technical request information according to their own privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

GRAMBOX may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes in application functionality, legal requirements, or data handling practices.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
